# Tonight: fridge & pantry helpers

Two tools that take *what you actually have* — not what a recipe blog imagines you bought — and hand back dinner options. Blurb note: **two lines each — what it is, why you'd flip to it tonight.**

## The picks

### [SuperCook](https://supercook.com/ "SuperCook — search recipes by ingredients you have")
Type in what's in your fridge (or just likely things: eggs, rice, frozen peas, canned tomatoes); it matches recipes you can actually make right now.
Tonight use: fastest zero-plan dinner finder on this list — the "Expired / Almost would be thrown out" filter is quietly brilliant before garbage day.

### [The Cook's Thesaurus](https://www.foodsubs.com/ "The Cook's Thesaurus — food substitutions encyclopedia")
A giant, calm encyclopedia of substitutions: for nearly any food, what to swap if it's missing, including cheaper versions.
Tonight use: when the recipe calls for the one thing you don't have — this turns "it's ruined" into "it's different, but it's dinner."
