# Budget-cooking blogs

People who cook dinner nightly on a real grocery budget and write down what actually worked. One photo per recipe, no diet-culture lectures, and per-serving cost where it matters. Blurb note: **two lines each — what it is, why you'd flip to it tonight.**

## The picks

### [Budget Bytes](https://www.budgetbytes.com/recipes/ "Recipe index of Budget Bytes")
The benchmark budget blog — hundreds of tested recipes with per-serving cost, plus pantry-stocking guides that reward reading once.
Tonight use: sort the index by ingredient you already own, pick the cheapest per-serving match, done. The comment sections often catch substitutions.

### [Frugal Feeding](https://www.frugalfeeding.com/ "Frugal Feeding — budget recipe blog")
A UK-based blog of genuinely frugal, from-scratch recipes where cost-cutting is the actual subject, not a tag.
Tonight use: strong on stretchy mains, soups, and bread-adjacent ideas that use cheap staples instead of special anything.

### [Cheap Recipe Blog](https://www.cheaprecipeblog.com/ "Cheap Recipe Blog")
Short, ingredient-cheap recipes with a knack for turning condiments and odd pantry goods into actual dinner.
Tonight use: the "what to do with half a can of X" energy is exactly what a Sunday-was-yesterday fridge demands.

### [Good Cheap Eats](https://goodcheapeats.com/ "Good Cheap Eats")
No-panic, home-cooking-on-a-budget writing with batch-cooking logic baked into nearly every recipe.
Tonight use: when tonight's dinner should also quietly feed Thursday — the structure of the recipes supports cooking once.

### [$5 Dinners](https://www.5dollardinners.com/ "$5 Dinners")
Long-running blog whose whole premise is in the name: feed the whole family dinner for about five dollars.
Tonight use: many recipes are price-point-anchored, so shop the loss-leaders and this page supplies the plan for the rest of the week.

### [Cooking on a Bootstrap](https://cookingonabootstrap.com/ "Cooking on a Bootstrap (UK, by Jack Monroe)")
Jack Monroe's stark-raving-honest writing about hunger budgets, with recipes measured in pennies and real quantities.
Tonight use: the UK staple-list recipes are a masterclass in flavor-per-penny; convert weights if needed and your own budget will notice.
