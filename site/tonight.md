# The Tonight page: dinner formulas, no other tabs needed

No photos, no stories about our grandmother, no cookie banner. These are formulas — swap freely, since almost every one is designed around "whatever's in the fridge." Pick one, cook it, you're done. Quantities are forgiving; salt at the end.

## The formulas

### 1. Garlicky noodles (aglio e olio, loosely)
Boil a small pasta shape. Meanwhile, warm lots of sliced garlic and a pinch of chili flakes in a good oil pour — no browning, gentle heat. Toss pasta in with a splash of pasta water and a hard squeeze of lemon or a sprinkle of vinegar. Optional: parmesan, frozen peas, canned tuna. Done in 15.

### 2. Beans-and-rice bowl
Cook or reheat rice. Warm a rinsed can of beans with onion, garlic, a spoon of tomato paste, and generous spice (cumin + smoked paprika is the workhorse). Pile on rice; top with anything crunchy (chip, peanut, carrot) and a squeeze of lime. Doubles easily; leftovers become lunch.

### 3. Skillet eggs with whatever's green
Wilt spinach/beans/kale in butter, then slide eggs in to fry or scramble right on top. Dinner in one pan. Toast on the side: bread under the broiler for two minutes, butter after. Any greens → this dinner.

### 4. Quesadilla upgrade
Tortilla + cheese + one real ingredient (leftover chicken, black beans, sautéed onion, canned green chilis). Fold, toast on both sides in a dry or barely buttered pan until crisp. Cut into wedges. The one-real-ingredient rule is what makes this dinner instead of a snack.

### 5. One-pot pasta
Everything lives in one pot: pasta, just-enough water or stock, halved cherry or chopped canned tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, salt. Boil ~10 minutes, stirring, until the water is absorbed into sauce. Add frozen veg in the last 2 minutes. There's no draining. It sounds fake and it works.

### 6. Congee / rice porridge sketch
Simmer 1 part rice in 8 parts water or broth, ~40 minutes (any broken rice works), until creamy. Stir in shredded leftover meat, a sliced egg, frozen peas, sesame oil, soy. The exact recipe varies by grandmother — the formula does not.

### 7. Tuna noodle classic, no casserole dish
Cook noodles. Stir together tuna, a big spoon of mayo or sour cream, a splash of the pasta water, mustard, frozen peas, black pepper; warm gently through. If you own their breadcrumbs, butter-toast a handful on top. Basically the comfort of the casserole without the oven heat.

### 8. Soup shortcut: onion + can
Soften an onion in butter. Pour in one big can of tomatoes (crushed, diced — whatever), one can of broth, simmer 15 minutes, blitz or don't. Add a can of beans to make it a meal. Add cream or don't. This exact move is equally good with carrots or broccoli — the inside of a vegetable pantry.

### 9. Stir-fry without the recipe
Hot pan, a neutral oil, hard veg first (carrots, broccoli stems, cabbage) for 3–4 minutes, soft veg next (peppers, mushrooms, frozen mixture) for 2. Push aside, scramble an egg in the space, then everything back with cooked rice or noodles plus soy + a spoon of sugar + garlic. Wrong vegetables do not exist here.

### 10. Toast salad (bread for dinner)
Toast thick bread hard. Rough-rub with a garlic clove. Top with oil, salt, tomato slices, canned sardines or white beans, a bit of anything pickled. Add a soft egg if in the house. It's dinner because it's on a plate and there's a protein.
