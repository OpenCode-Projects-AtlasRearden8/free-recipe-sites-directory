# Free Recipe Sites Directory

Genuinely free recipe sources, hand-picked and link-checked. No paywalls, no "free trial" tricks, no recipe riddled with pop-ups — just places that help you get dinner sorted.

**How to flip to dinner in under a minute:**

1. Open the [Tonight page](tonight.html) if you already roughly know what your pantry can do — cook straight from it.
2. Otherwise open the [fridge / pantry search tool](tonight-helpers.html) and type what you have.
3. Grab a deets-packed recipe: [budget blogs](budget-blogs.html) for the "how do I afford this", [government & extension nutrition sites](government-nutrition.html) for the "is this feeding my family right", [library & free archives](library-archives.html) when you want the deep cut.

## Categories

- **[Library & free-culture archives](library-archives.html)** — public-domain cookbooks, digital library menus, and open-recipe collections. Free in the "out of copyright, permanent on a library server" sense.
- **[Government & extension nutrition sites](government-nutrition.html)** — USDA, NHLBI, and university extension recipe hubs. Genuinely free, genuinely nutritionist-reviewed.
- **[Budget-cooking blogs](budget-blogs.html)** — people who cook every night on a real grocery budget and write down what actually worked.
- **[Text-first, no-photo & fast sites](no-photo-fast.html)** — zero photo essays, near-zero data. Perfect on bad connections and old phones.
- **[Tonight: fridge & pantry helpers](tonight-helpers.html)** — type your ingredients, get dinner ideas.
- **[The Tonight page](tonight.html)** — dinner formulas you can cook with no link open at all.

## Why this exists

Most "best recipe sites" round-ups rank sites by how good they look in a screenshot. This directory ranks by a different question: **can you flip to a workable dinner, tonight, for free, on a slow connection?** Among other things that's why a text-only 1998 archive sits next to a modern SNAP-Ed recipe database — both of them answer that question.

## Notes

- Every external link was live at build time and is re-checked by the included link-check script. Some libraries block datacenter traffic, so the checker treats un-fetchable-but-real sites as warnings, not failures — see the [README](../README.md).
- We promote no sites. No affiliate links, no sponsored anything.
- If a recipe site starts charging mid-browse or wants an account, close the tab — there is always a free copy of the same idea on one of these pages.
