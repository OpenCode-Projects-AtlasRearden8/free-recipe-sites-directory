# Government & extension nutrition sites

Your tax dollars paid for these. No ads, no sponsors, no "sponsored post" — just recipes and guidance that someone with a nutrition degree reviewed. Blurb note: **two lines each — what it is, why you'd flip to it tonight.**

## The picks

### [MyPlate Kitchen — USDA](https://www.myplate.gov/recipes "USDA MyPlate Kitchen recipe collection")
A big, free, ad-free database of tested healthy recipes with built-in nutrition facts, many on a budget and SNAP-friendly.
Tonight use: filter by ingredient or cost, get a recipe that already knows its nutrition numbers — no reverse-engineering needed.

### [SNAP-Ed Connection recipe search — USDA](https://snaped.fns.usda.gov/recipe-search "USDA FNS SNAP-Ed recipe database")
The recipe index behind SNAP nutrition education: cheap, standard-ingredient, family-scale recipes with cost and prep filters.
Tonight use: the narrow search (main ingredient + budget + minutes) is one of the fastest "I'm shopping right now" tools on this list.

### [Nutrition.gov cooking & meal planning](https://www.nutrition.gov/topics/shopping-cooking-and-meal-planning "Nutrition.gov hub for shopping, cooking and meal planning")
A hub page from the USDA portal that rounds up the government's cooking and planning resources in one clean list.
Tonight use: when you want *the* reference page rather than one recipe — it links healthy-cooking guides, food safety, and budget tips.

### [Food Hero recipes — Oregon State Extension](https://www.foodhero.org/recipes "Oregon State University Extension SNAP-Ed recipe site")
University extension recipe site with tested low-cost recipes, family-tested and sorted by ingredient, time, and cost.
Tonight use: the extension-test-recipe standard shows here: forgiving proportions, real pantry ingredients, no hard-to-find anything.

### [Spend Smart. Eat Smart. — Iowa State Extension](https://spendsmart.extension.iastate.edu/recipe/ "Iowa State University Extension budget cooking site")
Low-cost recipes with per-serving cost estimates, plus honest "is this actually cheaper" notes from extension educators.
Tonight use: the per-serving cost line next to each recipe makes sideways-between-similar-dinner decisions easy.

### [DASH Eating Plan — NHLBI](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/education/dash-eating-plan "NIH NHLBI DASH eating plan pages")
The NIH's heart-healthy DASH plan: sample menus, weekly planner goals, and heart-friendly recipe ideas — evidence-based, not fad.
Tonight use: when someone in the house is watching blood pressure or sodium, these are the dinners a cardiologist actually describes.
